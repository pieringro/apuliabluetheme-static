What's nearby ApuliaBlue?

Bari’s Old Town is a maze of charming alleys and ancient traditions. Just 6 km from the B&B...

Giovinazzo is a medieval gem overlooking the sea, perfect for a romantic stroll...

Castel del Monte is a masterpiece of medieval architecture. A UNESCO World Heritage site and a symbol of authentic Puglia.

Polignano a Mare is famous for its breathtaking cliffs and charming old town.

Monopoli is famous for its charming old town and stunning beaches.

Trani is famous for its cathedral by the sea and picturesque harbour.

Matera is famous for its Sassi, a UNESCO World Heritage site of ancient dwellings carved into the rock.

Alberobello is famous for its trulli, a UNESCO World Heritage site, typical circular dwellings built of stone.

Villa ApuliaBlue1 Bari: Your Seaside Oasis in Puglia

Dreaming of a vacation just steps from the sea? Our Villa ApuliaBlue1 in Bari is the answer! Nestled in a quiet residential complex with private access to a natural beach, it offers privacy and comfort for families and friends. Perfect for those seeking relaxation and adventure, it is the ideal base for exploring beautiful Puglia!

Why Choose Us? Benefits That Make the Difference

  • Top Location: Very close to Bari Airport (perfect for those arriving by plane!) and well connected to the city center (just 20 minutes by bus). Exploring Puglia has never been easier!
  • Exclusive Private Beach: Forget the crowds! Enjoy moments of pure relaxation on a natural rocky beach accessible only to you.
  • Free Private Parking: Arriving by car? No problem, your spot is guaranteed.
  • Absolute Comfort All Year Round: Air conditioning in every room, inverter heating for a pleasant stay in every season.

Spaces and Comfort: Your Home Away From Home

The villa is designed for your maximum well-being, with two floors of pure comfort:

  • Fully Equipped Kitchen: Prepare your favorite dishes with latest-generation appliances (dishwasher, microwave, oven, fridge/freezer, coffee machine, electric kettle upon request).
  • Outdoor Relaxation: Enjoy lunches and dinners in the private garden, relax on the two large terraces, or admire the breathtaking sea view from your room’s balcony.

Ricasoli27 in Mottola: Your Authentic Escape into the Soul of Puglia

Are you looking for a genuine and immersive travel experience, far from the usual tourist trails? Ricasoli27 in the village of Mottola is your ideal retreat. Immersed in the tranquility of a hill 387 meters above sea level, Mottola welcomes you with breathtaking views and a millennial history of the Messapian and Magna Graecia civilizations waiting to be discovered.

If you wish to explore the Apulian hinterland, experiencing slow tourism in contact with nature and the rich local culture, our apartment is the perfect choice for you. Here, maximum comfort and total privacy await you for an unforgettable vacation.

You can also enjoy biker tours; details are available at the property.

Your Relaxation Space: The Ricasoli27 Apartment

Designed to accommodate up to 6 people, Ricasoli27 offers ideal spaces for families or groups of friends. Located on the ground floor of an early 20th-century building with cross-vaulted ceilings, every detail has been curated to ensure a comfortable and relaxing stay, inviting you to regenerate after a day of exploration.

The apartment features:

  • 1 double bedroom: Your nest of tranquility.
  • Living room with sofa bed: A convivial space for evenings together.
  • Anteroom with an additional single sofa bed.
  • Full bathroom with shower and laundry facilities: Every comfort at your disposal.
  • Outdoor space: Your personal oasis to enjoy relaxation in the open air in total privacy.
