Awakening in Puglia

A journey through authentic flavors, from 1200 to today

We invite you to start your day by celebrating the richness of our traditions. Featuring locally sourced products, ancient grains, and care for every dietary need.

Treasures from the Wood-Fired Oven

Since 1200

The heart of our breakfast beats to the rhythm of fresh bread, baked in an ancient wood-fired oven dating back to 1200. A flavor that spans centuries.

  • Focaccia Barese: Soft, golden, topped with cherry tomatoes and olives.
  • Taralli and Frise: The crunch of tradition.
  • Durum Wheat Bread: Natural leavening and an intense aroma.

Flavors of the Masseria

A celebration of local, artisanal, and genuine dairy products.

  • Canestrato Cheese: Cow or sheep milk, ranging from fresh to aged.
  • Fresh Ricotta: Cow, buffalo, or sheep milk—perfect with honey.
  • Fior di Latte: The freshness of freshly made Apulian mozzarella.
